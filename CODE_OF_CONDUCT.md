# Code of Conduct

Blocks is made for people who want a simple way to build websites, and by people who care about that. We want everyone here, beginners first, to feel welcome.

## What we expect

- **Be kind and patient.** Every question is a fair question. Nobody was born knowing what a "slot" is.
- **Be respectful.** Disagree with ideas; never attack people. Assume good intentions.
- **Be helpful.** Explain in plain words, point to the docs, and share what you've learned.
- **Be inclusive.** Welcome people of every background, identity, language and level of experience.

## What we don't accept

- Insults, harassment, threats, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexual language or images, and unwanted attention.
- Discriminatory jokes or remarks.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly derailing discussions or ignoring a request to stop.

## Where it applies

In the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and anywhere you represent the project.

## If something goes wrong

Tell the maintainers privately. On an issue, a pull request or a comment, open the **…** menu, choose **Report content**, then **Report to repository admins**: only the maintainers see the report. For anything that isn't about a single comment, use the repository's **Security** tab → **Report a vulnerability** and start the title with "Conduct": it's private too. We'll listen, keep it confidential and respond fairly, from a friendly word to removing comments or banning someone from the project, depending on what happened.

Thank you for helping keep Blocks a good place.
